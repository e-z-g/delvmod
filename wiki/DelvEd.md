
The original editor for [Delver](Delver)-based games. It was not intended for general use, and was reportedly buggy and somewhat incomplete. Besides the facilities for making [Delver Archives](Delver-Archives) that can be inferred, it is also known to have included a level editor and a compiler for scripts. (Scripts are only stored in the scenario file in their compiled form.) It is a classic Mac OS application and as far as is known, no one even attempted to Carbonize it. 

Over the years, many people have asked for it to be released, to no avail. Apart from the objections that it was difficult to use, undocumented and buggy, there was a concern that people would use it to bypass Cythera's registration checks if it were available.
