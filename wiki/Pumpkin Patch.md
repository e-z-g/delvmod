
The Pumpkin Patch is a patch to [Cythera](Cythera), for use with [Magpie](Magpie). It is a simple graphical modification that introduces an Autumn/Halloween theme. Patches for Cythera are [Delver Archives](Delver-Archives). It has no resource fork, it is purely a Delver Archive.
