[Cythera](http://www.ambrosiasw.com/games/cythera/) is a role playing game for the classic Macintosh, written by [Glenn Andreas](http://www.gandreas.com/) and published by Ambrosia software. It is based on the Delver engine, which was never used for any other games. It had a very advanced and Mac-like interface, which, unfortunately, proved to be its undoing with the release of Mac OS X -- Cythera did not work well under the "Classic" compatiblity system, and proved impractical to port to Carbon. Nowadays, it can be [played on modern computers](Cythera-on-Mac-Emulators) only by use of an emulator, such as Basilisk II. 

For many years, the only "modding" available for Cythera was through [editing of save files](Cythera-Save-File-Hacks), but in 2014, [software able to interpret the main data file](Narthex) was developed, followed, in 2015, by a third-party editor interpolatable with the Delver engine, [redelv](redelv).
