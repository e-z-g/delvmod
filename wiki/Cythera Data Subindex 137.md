
Why these icons aren't stored as [Delver Compressed Graphics](Delver-Compressed-Graphics) is unclear -- perhaps they are accessed in some way through the scripting system, and it has no access to the graphics decompression API? The stuff in resources 1Axx (skill name, description, etc) seems to all come from scripts that display (or return?) strings. 

Many of the later icons are blank. Perhaps these correspond to skills (e.g. Cooking, Alchemy, Defense) that do not have (visible) icons. 

The icons can be read with the [delv](delv) example program icon_view.py
